Forage Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

Forage helps you learn words you come across while reading. This page explains what the Forage Chrome extension and web app collect, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a password (stored hashed by our authentication provider).
  • Words you save: the word, the sentence around it, the page title and address of the page, and when you saved it. Forage only reads a page when you save a word from it (with the right-click menu or the keyboard shortcut). It never reads pages in the background or records your browsing history.
  • Practice: the sentences you write when practicing, how they were graded, and your review schedule.
  • Settings and reminders: your preferences, and, if you turn on phone reminders, a push subscription for your browser.

How we use it

Only to run Forage: to look up what a saved word means in its sentence, to schedule practice, and to grade your practice sentences. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use it for anything unrelated to Forage.

Who processes it

  • Supabase hosts the database and sign-in.
  • Anthropic (Claude) receives saved words with their sentences, and your practice sentences, to explain meanings and grade answers. Anthropic does not use API data to train its models.
  • Vercel hosts the web app.

Stored on your device

The extension keeps your sign-in session, words saved while offline (until they sync), and reminder timing in Chrome's local extension storage.

Deleting your data

Email us at the address below to delete your account. Deleting the account removes your saved words, practice history, settings, and reminder subscriptions.
